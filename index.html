The studio

Made small.
Played big.

Indiee is one developer in Pakistan with a long list of game ideas and a gumdrop for a mascot. Every game we make follows three rules.

01

Easy in, hard out.

Start a run in seconds. Look up and it's been hours. Simple to learn, with enough depth to keep you coming back.

02

Made in-house, all of it.

Art, music, code and the tools that build them. When it's one person, every pixel gets looked at twice.

03

Better together.

Co-op is built in from day one. Share a room code and bring up to three friends along.

Mascot

Meet Dee.

Dee is a gumdrop monster with two horns and a big pair of eyes: the "ee" of Indiee, made into a face. Cheeky, never scary, and usually up to something.
