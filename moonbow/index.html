How it plays

One thumb. Endless sparkle.

Every fight is a little dance: slip between the shots, plant your feet, let the arrows fly. Then do it again, a step braver.

  1. 1

    Move to dodge

    Drag anywhere to run. Slimes spit, beetles charge, owlets dive. Glowing marks warn you before anything lands.

  2. 2

    Stop to shoot

    Let go and your hero locks on to the nearest foe and fires on their own. Standing still is how you hit hardest.

  3. 3

    Catch a moon-gift

    Every level brings a moon-gift: three skill cards to choose from. Stack them into a build that's yours alone.

Meet the party

Nine heroes. One moonlit path.

Bows, staves, maces and kites. Each hero brings a passive of their own and a signature move that fires by itself, and every one of them grows with the same Moon Path.

Rare

Wren Archer

Crescent Volley. Every sixth volley fans out five extra arrows.

Rare

Oakley Tank

Thornroot. Hits harder as health drops, and thorns strike back at anyone close.

Rare

Rime Frost mage

Snowfan. A burst of frost that chills everything nearby.

Epic

Kael Warrior

Brand Charge. Charged swings land twice as hard and come back quickly.

Epic

Pip Summoner

Squall Dive. A kite familiar circles Pip, then dives straight through a line of foes.

Epic

Ember Fire mage

Lantern Burst. A lantern bursts on the target and sets the crowd ablaze.

Epic

Mending Seeds. Steady healing, and one extra rise from defeat every run.

The journey

Eight chapters under one moon.

190 waves from the garden gate to the attic of dreams. Every chapter has its own ground to read, its own monsters and its own boss.

  1. 1

    Moonlit Garden

    Moonpools glow between the flagstones. Mind the slimes.

    10 waves · Boss: the Toad King

  2. 2

    Hushwood Glade

    Spore rings bloom among the hedges and pillars.

    20 waves · Bosses: Mother Gloop, Hollow Stump

  3. 3

    Lanternmere Shallows

    Moonwater slows every step you take through it.

    20 waves · Bosses: Lantern Heron, Grumblegourd

  4. 4

    Pumpkin Vale

    Market stalls crumble under fire, and the candy cart rolls through.

    20 waves · Bosses: Market Grumblegourd, Granny Sheaf

  5. 5

    Cinderstep Kilns

    Ember vents flare one after another. Read the rhythm.

    30 waves · Boss: Kiln Spirit Hearthe

  6. 6

    Frostveil Orchard

    Frost glaze keeps you sliding long after you let go.

    30 waves · Boss: the Frost Matron

  7. 7

    Starfall Causeway

    A narrow lane high above a sea of clouds.

    30 waves · Boss: the Causeway Archer

  8. 8

    Dreamloom Attic

    Carpet runners carry you along whether you like it or not.

    30 waves · Boss: the Tinker Dreamweaver

Beyond the chapters

Climb higher. Come back tomorrow.

Endless

Stairway of Stars

One room, one floor, one step closer to the sky. Every floor starts with fresh skill cards, every fifth is guarded by a Sentinel, and the stairway never runs out.

Daily

Daily Trials

Moonsilver Burrow: sixty seconds to break as many runaway ore carts as you can, combo after combo. Hero Trial: a different hero on each trial day, yours to play even before you own them.

Our promise

Fair skies, clear odds.

Moonbow is free to play. We want every chest and every summon to feel like a treat, never a trick.

  • %Odds up front. The chances for every chest and summon are shown before you open it.
  • ★Luck that adds up. Pity counters are on screen and carry over between banners.
  • ☾A free summon every day. Just for showing up.
  • ✓No account needed. Your progress is saved right on your phone.
