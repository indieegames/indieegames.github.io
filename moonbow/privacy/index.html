This policy explains what information Moonbow ("the game") handles, and why. Moonbow is made by Indiee ("we", "us"), an independent game studio based in Pakistan. Contact: hello.indiee@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. We don't run servers that store it.
  • You can choose to watch ads for in-game rewards. Our ad partners collect device information to show and measure them.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information we handle

Progress and settings. Your heroes, gear, currencies, chapters and settings are stored on your device only. We don't receive a copy and the game has no cloud save, so if you uninstall the game or clear its storage, your progress is gone.

Rewarded ads. The game offers ads you can choose to watch for a reward (for example extra currency or a second chance in a run). Ads are served by Unity LevelPlay, which works with other ad networks including Google AdMob. These services may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address (and the approximate location it implies), device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource): unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice in the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchases. You can buy in-game items (such as gems, packs and passes) with real money through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Google tells the game which items you bought, and keeps your purchase history under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

Chests and summons. Some items and heroes come from chests and summons with random results. The odds of every result are shown in the game before you open or pull. This happens on your device and sends us nothing.

Diagnostics. The game doesn't send us usage statistics or crash reports. The ad services and Google Play may collect diagnostics for their own purposes, as described in their policies.

This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ads. Its host keeps the usual short-lived server logs (IP address, time and page requested) for security, and the page fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address (policies.google.com/privacy).

Children

Moonbow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to its use. We hold no account or profile for you: your progress is on your device, and you can erase it in the game (Settings, then Data, then Reset progress). For data held by the ad partners or Google, use the links above or your device's privacy settings. You can also email us and we will help.

Security and retention

We keep no personal information about you on our own servers. No method of transfer or storage is perfectly secure.

Changes

We will update this page when the game changes how it handles information (for example if we add an online feature), and change the "Last updated" date.
