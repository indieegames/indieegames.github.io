This policy explains what information Last Lantern ("the game") and other games by Indiee ("we", "us") handle, and why. Indiee is an independent game studio based in Pakistan. Contact: hello.indiee@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your progress is saved on your device and, if you choose, in your platform's cloud save.
  • The free mobile and web versions show ads. Our ad partners collect device information to show and measure ads.
  • If you play online co-op, the nickname you choose is seen by the people in your party.
  • On CrazyGames, if you're logged in, your CrazyGames username and best scores appear on the in-game leaderboards.

Information we handle

Progress and settings. Your save (unlocks, gold, records, settings) is stored on your device. If cloud saves are available on your platform, a copy is stored by that platform: Google Play Games (Saved Games) on Android, Steam Cloud on Steam, or the CrazyGames account on CrazyGames. We don't operate servers that store your save.

Online co-op. When you host or join an online party, the game connects through our relay server, which forwards game data between the players in real time. It handles your chosen nickname, the room code, your game version and your IP address (needed to send data to you). Gameplay data is not stored. The server's log records when rooms open and close, the nicknames in them and the IP address of the player who opened the room; these logs are kept for up to 30 days for running and protecting the service, then deleted. Don't use your real name or personal details as a nickname.

Leaderboards. On CrazyGames, when you're logged in to your CrazyGames account, your best scores (Endless Night times, most kills in one night, enemies and bosses slain) are sent to our server with your CrazyGames user ID and username, and other players see your username and scores on the in-game leaderboards. Guests aren't posted. We keep leaderboard entries while the leaderboards run; email us to have yours removed. On Steam and Google Play, scores go to those platforms' own leaderboards under your Steam or Google Play Games profile, under their privacy policies.

Ads (free versions on Android, iOS and web). We use these ad services, which may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • On CrazyGames: the CrazyGames ad system (crazygames.com/privacy-policy)

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads and you can change your choice in the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. The paid Steam version has no ads.

Notifications (Android). To send you news about the game (updates, events, new maps), the Android version uses Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging, which gives your device an anonymous push token and a Firebase installation ID. They identify the app install, not you, and are used only to deliver notifications (firebase.google.com/support/privacy). Android asks before showing notifications; you can turn them off any time in your device's settings for Last Lantern. Firebase Analytics is switched off in the game.

Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by the store (Google Play, Apple App Store, Steam, or Xsolla on CrazyGames). We never see your card or payment details; the store tells the game which items you own.

Analytics and crash reports. The game itself does not send analytics or crash reports to us. The ad and platform services above may collect diagnostics for their own purposes as described in their policies.

This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ads. Its host keeps the usual short-lived server logs (IP address, time and page requested) for security, and the page fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address (policies.google.com/privacy). The trailer plays from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and only after you press play; from then on YouTube's privacy policy applies to it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can play without online co-op and without signing in to any cloud service. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to its use. Because we keep no accounts, the only personal information we hold is the short-lived co-op log and, on CrazyGames, your leaderboard entries (described above); email us and we will delete what we can link to you. For data held by ad partners or platforms, use the links above or your device's privacy settings.

Security and retention

We keep only what the game needs, for as short a time as possible: co-op logs for up to 30 days, CrazyGames leaderboard entries while the leaderboards run, nothing else on our servers. No method of transfer or storage is perfectly secure.

Changes

We will update this page when the game changes how it handles information, and change the "Last updated" date.
