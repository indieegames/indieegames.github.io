Last Lantern has no accounts, so there is very little to delete. Here is everything the game or its partners may hold, and how to remove it.

Ask us to delete your co-op data

The only personal data we (Indiee) keep is the log of our online co-op server: the nickname you used and, if you hosted a party, your IP address. These logs are deleted automatically after 30 days.

To have them deleted sooner, email hello.indiee@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and include:

  1. the co-op nickname(s) you used, and
  2. roughly when you played online (dates).

We delete the matching log entries within 30 days and reply when it's done. We keep nothing else about you.

Delete your progress

Your progress is stored on your device: open Settings → Reset progress in the game, or uninstall it. If you use cloud saves, also delete the copy kept by your platform:

  • Android: Google Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data (or delete just this game's data).
  • Steam: Steam Cloud copies follow your Steam account (see Steam support).
  • CrazyGames: saves follow your CrazyGames account (see CrazyGames' privacy settings).

Purchases are kept by the store (Google Play, Apple, Steam, CrazyGames/Xsolla) so you can restore them; contact the store to remove purchase records.

Advertising data

Ads in the free version come from Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), Unity Ads and Google AdMob, which use your device's advertising ID and IP-based approximate location. To reset or delete your advertising ID on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (wording varies by phone). To ask the ad partners to delete data they hold, use their privacy pages linked from our Privacy Policy at https://indieegames.github.io/privacy.

Questions? Write to hello.indiee@gmail.com.
